/03 How To Carry It

Six tips
to pack like
you fly often.

From the editor's notebook.

01
The 5-4-3-2-1 Method

Pack the math.

For a 4-night trip: 5 tops, 4 underwear, 3 bottoms, 2 pairs of shoes, 1 jacket. Fits exactly into 47L without compressing. Wear the heaviest items on the flight.

02
The Compression Trick

Roll, then cinch.

Roll non-formal items (tees, denim, loungewear) instead of folding. Saves 30% space. Then strap the X-compression band tight. Adds room for two extra pairs of shoes.

03
Front Pocket Priority

The grab list.

Only five things go in the front: laptop, charger, headphones, passport, hotel address. Nothing else. Pull the laptop at security in three seconds without disturbing the main pack.

04
The Shoe Hack

Stuff, don't waste.

Empty shoes are dead space. Fill them with socks, undergarments, chargers, or a rolled-up belt. Adds an entire sub-compartment without taking a square inch more.

05
The Weight Rule

Bottom-heavy wins.

Heaviest items go at the wheel end (the bottom when standing upright). Keeps the bag balanced when telescoping. The spinner wheels glide better with weight near them, not the handle.

06
The Lock Memory

Birthday math.

Set the 3-dial lock combo to the last three digits of a number you'll never forget (date of birth, anniversary, last 3 of phone). Resist the urge to use 1-2-3.
